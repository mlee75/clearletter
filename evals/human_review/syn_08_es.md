## What this letter says
You came to the hospital on 14 October 2026 after a fall at home. You hit your head. You did not lose consciousness.

- The X-ray of the right hip does not show a fracture.
- The CT scan of the head (CT) does not show bleeding inside the head.
- Your blood pressure drops when you stand up (postural hypotension). Lying down it was 142/80. Standing up it was 108/66.
- The amlodipine may be the cause of that drop in blood pressure. It is possible, it is not certain.

## What you have to do
1. Stop taking the amlodipine.
2. Do not stop your other medicines. Keep taking them.
3. Get up slowly.
4. Before standing up, sit for one minute on the edge of the bed.
5. Wait for a letter from the Falls Prevention Clinic with your appointment.
6. Your family doctor (GP) will measure your blood pressure again lying down and standing up.

## Dates and medicines
- 14 October 2026: you were seen at the hospital.
- 4 weeks after the visit: your family doctor measures your blood pressure again lying down and standing up.
- Amlodipine 10 milligrams: it is stopped. It may be lowering your blood pressure when you stand up.
- All the other medicines: continue the same.

## Questions for your doctor
- Why is the amlodipine being stopped?
- When will I get the appointment from the Falls Prevention Clinic?
- How should I get out of bed to be safer?

## When to ask for urgent help
If in the next few days you start to have a headache that gets worse, vomiting, confusion or a lot of sleepiness, call 999 or go to the emergency department (A&E).

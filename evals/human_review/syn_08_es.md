Northbridge General Hospital - Emergency Department and Acute Medicine

Patient: Mrs Olga Petrenko, age 81
Seen: 14/10/2026

Reason for attendance: Fall at home. Hit head. No loss of consciousness.

Investigations:
- X-ray of the right hip: no fracture.
- CT head: no evidence of intracranial haemorrhage (no bleeding inside the head).

Plan:
- STOP amlodipine 10 mg. It may be making your blood pressure drop when you stand.
- Continue all other medicines.
- Referred to the Falls Prevention Clinic. They will write to you with an appointment.
- GP to recheck lying and standing blood pressure in 4 weeks.

Advice: Stand up slowly. Sit on the edge of the bed for a minute before standing.

If you develop a worsening headache, vomiting, confusion or drowsiness in the next few days, call 999 or go to A&E.

Dr S. Kowalczyk, Acute Medicine

# syn_04 (es)

## Original letter

```
Riverside Diabetic Eye Screening Programme

Dear Mr Tomasz Nowak,

You are invited for your diabetic eye screening. This is a test that everyone with diabetes aged 12 and over is offered every year. It checks the back of the eyes for damage caused by diabetes (diabetic retinopathy).

This is a screening test. It does not diagnose other eye problems, and it does not replace your regular eye test at an optician.

Please book your appointment by 15 January 2027 by calling 01632 960 377.

We will put drops in your eyes. The drops will make your vision blurred for 4 to 6 hours. You must not drive until your vision is back to normal. Bring sunglasses if you have them.

Bring your glasses or contact lenses and a list of your medicines.

Screening Programme Office
```

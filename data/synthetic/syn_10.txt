Northbridge Children's Assessment Unit - Discharge Advice

Child: Lina Haddad, age 2 years, weight 12 kg
Seen: 20/10/2026

Diagnosis: Gastroenteritis (tummy bug) with mild dehydration. Lina drank well on the unit and passed urine before going home. No blood tests were needed.

Treatment at home:
- Give small amounts of fluid often: 5 ml every 5 minutes if she is vomiting, then more as she keeps it down.
- Oral rehydration solution (Dioralyte) can be used. Keep breastfeeding or giving her usual milk.
- For fever or discomfort: paracetamol oral suspension 120 mg/5 ml. Give 7.5 ml, up to 4 times in 24 hours. Leave at least 4 hours between doses.
- Do not give anti-diarrhoea medicines to your child.

Diarrhoea usually stops within 5 to 7 days. Vomiting usually stops within 3 days.

Bring Lina back or call 111 if she has fewer than 4 wet nappies in 24 hours, is very sleepy, has blood in her poo or cannot keep any fluids down.
Call 999 if she is hard to wake up.
